How we work

Four stages, and your team is involved in each one.

  1. Listen

    We start with your goals, your team, your constraints, and what you've already tried.

  2. Map

    We chart how work and decisions flow today and where they stall.

  3. Build

    We design and set up the fixes alongside your team, not around them.

  4. Hand over

    Your team owns the result, with documentation and a check-in to confirm it holds.
